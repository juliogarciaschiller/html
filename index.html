Spaces Reservation

Room layout options

These are the room setups available for events. Review each layout and choose the one that best fits your event when you submit your request in the portal.

Table Chair Screen / front of the room

01

Banquet

Round tables for 8–10 guests spread across the room, encouraging conversation within each group.

Ideal for

  • Dinners & galas
  • Networking
  • Group workshops

02

Hollow Square

Tables arranged as a closed square with seating on the outside and an open space in the middle.

Ideal for

  • Committees
  • Round-table discussions
  • Debates

03

Classroom

Rows of tables facing the front of the room, with seating on one side so everyone can take notes.

Ideal for

  • Training sessions
  • Lectures
  • Exams

04

Theater

Rows of chairs facing the stage or screen, without tables. Offers the highest seating capacity.

Ideal for

  • Presentations
  • Conferences
  • Ceremonies

05

U-Shape

Tables set out in a U, open towards the presenter or screen, so participants see each other.

Ideal for

  • Interactive workshops
  • Presentations
  • Video calls

06

Conference

A single central table with seating all around it, for focused discussion among a small group.

Ideal for

  • Board meetings
  • Team meetings
  • Interviews

Need a different setup? Mention it in the description of your request and we will review it with you. Final capacity depends on the room selected.
